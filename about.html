About us

A small shop on the corner

Cup & Corner is a neighborhood coffee shop built around a simple idea: good coffee, honest prices, and a place to sit for as long as you like. We serve espresso drinks, tea and baked goods every day, and sell whole beans for brewing at home.

The coffee

We pick beans for balance and sweetness, and dial in the espresso every morning before opening.

The room

Window seats, a long shared table, and free Wi-Fi for anyone who wants to work or read.
